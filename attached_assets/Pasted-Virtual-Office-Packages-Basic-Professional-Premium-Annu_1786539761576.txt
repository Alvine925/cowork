Required Documents

Because you're providing a business address and handling business correspondence, I'd make the onboarding requirements clear from the beginning.

For registered companies

We require:

Certificate of Incorporation/Registration
Company registration documents
Valid ID/passport for at least two directors
Company/business contact details
Details of the authorized person managing the account

For sole proprietors or other business structures, you can define separate requirements depending on the registration documents available.
